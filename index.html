      How to play

      Cooking

      Slice
      Aim along the dashed guides and click or tap. Even slices score best.
      Crack
      Tap when the needle sits in the green band, then tap again to open.
      Pour
      Hold, and let go inside the green band.
      Stir
      Drag slow circles around the bowl or hold Space.
      Fry
      Wait for golden, then tap the pan to flip or take it off.
      Boil
      Drop it in, lift it out in the green band.
      Season
      Tap to shake. Stop in the band and press Done.
      Plate
      Drag each item onto its outline, or tap item then plate.

      Restaurant

      Server
      Tap a guest in the queue, then a table. Tap tables to take orders, serve and collect. Tap dishes on the pickup counter to carry them (two at a time). Repeating the same action earns a chain bonus.
      Chef
      Pick a ticket from the rail and cook it. Faster and better dishes earn bigger tips.
      Owner
      Both jobs. Tap the stove or a ticket to cook; the room slows while you do.

      Keys

      Space / Enter
      Main action in every cooking step
      Arrows
      Move the knife, change heat
      1 to 6
      Act on a table (server and owner); Q picks the next guest, P picks up from the pickup counter, K cooks (owner)
      Esc
      Pause
